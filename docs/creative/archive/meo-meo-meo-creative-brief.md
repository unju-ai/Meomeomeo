# meo meo meo

Creative direction and research brief · 5 September 2026 · Concept, not a token offering

## Recommendation

Build a talking-cat comedy property around **three cats sharing one braincell**. Their household is the entire universe. They discuss a closed door like a geopolitical crisis and an empty bowl like a banking collapse. The art feels collectible because the characters are recognizable, well authored, and worth returning to.

Brand line: **Three cats. One shared braincell.**

Recurring punchline: **404: thought not found.**

Working assumption: English dialogue for an international audience, with “meo meo meo” as an original three-note vocal signature. Test Vietnamese dialogue as a separate localized version once the format works. Audience and language remain open decisions.

## What the research suggests

These are qualitative design inferences from selected cases, not proof of virality or a forecast of NFT prices. Successful historical projects are subject to survivorship bias.

| Reference | Observed pattern | Application to Meo Meo Meo |
|---|---|---|
| CryptoPunks | A tightly constrained portrait system supports many recognizable identities; its history and community form part of its cultural significance. | Keep the cat silhouette, crop, and visual language consistent. Differentiate personalities through small meaningful changes. A new collection cannot reproduce historical provenance by copying pixel art. |
| Pudgy Penguins | The brand describes using GIFs, stickers, and memes to reach people who enjoy the characters without knowing the NFT project. | Make reaction images useful in everyday chats. Someone should love the cats before knowing a token exists. |
| Oiia and Maxwell | Documented cat meme formats traveled through repeated motions, sound associations, and remixing. | Give people a repeatable original sound, an easily isolated character, and a joke structure they can adapt. Do not assume the existing cat footage or audio is available to license. |

Sources: [CryptoPunks / NODE](https://nodefoundation.com/cryptopunks), [Pudgy Penguins recap](https://media.pudgypenguins.com/post/one-year-recap), [Oiia history](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/oo-ee-a-e-a-cat-remixes), [Maxwell history](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/maxwell-the-cat-spinning-cat).

NFT culture combines public identity, belonging, provenance, scarcity, collecting, and speculation. Our proposed emphasis is identity and participation: fans pick the cat that represents them and reuse its reactions. Scarcity can describe an edition; it cannot create demand by itself. Avoid manufactured sales, invented endorsements, price promises, and misleading rarity claims.

## Cast and comedy rules

| Cat | Visual | Voice | Comic function |
|---|---|---|---|
| MEO | Tangerine loaf, slightly lifted chin | Fast, completely certain | Announces an absurd explanation |
| ME | Ivory loaf, flat stare | Quiet, economical, deadpan | Asks the obvious question |
| MO | Charcoal loaf, slightly misaligned gaze | Delayed, soft, oddly solemn | Answers a different question and breaks reality |

All three share tiny triangular ears, wide-set eyes, a little flat mouth, and one cobalt square on the forehead: the visual “braincell.” These names are provisional until audience testing. Distinct voices matter more than viewers remembering three similar names.

Use recurring relationships, not disconnected random words. Every episode has one understandable situation, one escalation, and a payoff. Deliver the payoff in the same clip. The comedy comes from serious delivery applied to cat logic.

## Short-form production format

Starting hypothesis: vertical videos, 12–20 seconds, usually two speakers and one reaction. Test this range rather than treating it as an algorithm rule.

1. First second: start mid-crisis with a readable cat face and a concrete line. No opening logo.
2. Seconds 1–5: one cat states its theory; another exposes the contradiction.
3. Seconds 5–12: an ordinary object acquires absurd significance.
4. Final seconds: a deadpan reaction and a cut that naturally reconnects to the opening.

Use simple mouth swaps and restrained motion so the same character remains recognizable. Large sentence-case captions, strong contrast, and one line of emphasis at a time. Create an original three-note “meo meo meo” sound and keep music underneath speech. Use pauses as part of the rhythm; constant shouting will flatten the jokes.

Release clean clips without platform watermarks. Prepare isolated character PNGs, silent reaction loops, caption templates, and the original audio for audience remixing with clear reuse terms. Invite alternate dialogue and recurring prop suggestions. Keep participation available without buying anything.

## Six pilot scripts

### 1. Rent

MEO: “The human said rent is due.”

ME: “We pay rent.”

MEO: “With what?”

MO, after a pause: “Presence.”

Cut to all three occupying the entire sofa. Human offscreen: “Rent is due.”

### 2. Door

MEO: “Open the door. It is urgent.”

Door opens. Nobody moves.

ME: “Are we going?”

MO: “We requested options.”

Door begins closing. MEO: “Open the door.”

### 3. Bowl

MEO: “We have no food.”

ME: “There is food around the edge.”

MO: “Those are international waters.”

Slow push toward the tiny visible patch at the center of the bowl.

### 4. Braincell

MEO: “Who has the braincell?”

ME: “You booked it.”

MEO: “For when?”

MO: “Yesterday.”

All three stare. A tiny notification sound. MEO: “Who has the braincell?”

### 5. Gravity

MEO, beside a cup: “I have concerns about gravity.”

ME: “You tested it.”

MEO knocks the cup offscreen.

MO: “Peer reviewed.”

### 6. Box

ME: “The human bought us a luxury bed.”

MEO, inside its shipping box: “This one?”

ME: “No.”

MO, also in box: “Then why does it fit my entire career?”

## First 30 days

Days 1–3: lock character proportions and voices; create front/side views and six mouth shapes; make the six pilots. Test face recognition at a 48-pixel crop and caption readability on a phone.

Days 4–10: publish one or two genuinely distinct clips daily as production allows. Rotate household crisis, braincell office, and object courtroom formats. Prepare twelve reaction images from the strongest moments.

Days 11–20: build sequels to the two best formats. Test one creative variable at a time across comparable clips: opening line, pause length, or caption treatment. This is directional testing, not a randomized experiment. Track each platform separately.

Days 21–30: turn strong audience replies into credited dialogue prompts; publish a weekly compilation; test interest in collecting the art without implying future returns. Decide whether the characters justify more production before deciding a mint size.

Measure stayed-to-watch, average view duration, average percentage viewed, shares per 1,000 views, follows per 1,000 views, and returning viewers where available. Compare seven-day medians for similar lengths, not only the single largest hit. YouTube documents viewed-versus-swiped-away and watch metrics in its [Shorts analytics guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12942217?co=YOUTUBE._YTVideoType%3Dshorts&hl=en) and [content performance documentation](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12220281?co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop&hl=en-GB).

Suggested internal decision rule, not an industry benchmark: make three follow-ups when a format repeatedly exceeds the channel's own median share rate and watch percentage. Rework formats that repeatedly trail both. Check comments for people quoting the characters; that is useful qualitative evidence of a recognizable voice.

## Art direction: collectible deadpan

Rounded, almost rectangular loaf bodies; tiny ears; exaggerated empty space between eyes; microscopic mouth. Matte painted-resin texture, softened edges, museum-like lighting, and calm compositions. The facial expression supplies the absurdity. The quality of execution supplies the collectible feel.

Palette: paper ivory #F3EFE5, ink #252725, tangerine #E88845, celadon #B7C9BB, faded salmon #D9A69B, cobalt #3154D5. Keep the cobalt braincell square invariant. No branded accessories or existing collection silhouettes.

Use the same models in three outputs: restrained gallery portraits, low-complexity talking animation, and isolated reaction stickers. Produce a model sheet and animation-ready master after the concept is approved; the generated board is an exploration, not a production rig or final collection.

Traits should express a story: posture, attention level, familiar household prop, material treatment, episode origin. Keep most pieces visually quiet and restrict each portrait to one dominant variation. Hand-curate every combination. A rare treatment might be a translucent “signal lost” edition, but common pieces must remain desirable as art.

Do not pick 10,000 items because older projects did. Start with a 24-image internal art study. Test which portraits people choose without showing rarity labels. Decide supply after establishing collector interest and production cost; publish any final supply, allocation, trait methodology, and rights clearly before selling.

Archive full-resolution masters, authorship records, generation/edit history, final prompts, and the collection's metadata policy. Define exactly what token holders may do with the artwork. A purchased token does not by itself answer the copyright/license question. Design production and operating budgets to work without assumed resale royalties.

## Robinhood and the “stocks” premise

Robinhood announced Robinhood Chain public mainnet on July 1, 2026. It describes a permissionless Arbitrum-based environment and stock tokens available to eligible users through Robinhood Wallet. That supports investigating a deployment on the chain; it does not establish a trading-app listing or creator stock reward. [Official launch announcement](https://robinhood.com/us/en/newsroom/robinhood-accelerates-global-expansion-robinhood-chain-mainnet-stock-tokens-agentic-trading/?lang=en).

Robinhood's newer Stock Tokens are tokenized debt securities issued by Robinhood Assets (Jersey) Limited. They provide economic exposure to underlying securities without ownership rights in the underlying issuer; geographic restrictions apply. The older EU Classic Stock Tokens have a different derivative-contract structure. Neither description means a third-party meme coin automatically gives its issuer or buyers stocks. [Current Stock Tokens](https://robinhood.com/rhj/stocktokens/), [Classic Stock Tokens FAQ](https://robinhood.com/eu/en/support/articles/stock-tokens-faq/).

The exact platform or program meant by “gives us stocks” remains unverified. Obtain its official link before evaluating rewards or incorporating this into public messaging. Treat chain deployment, wallet visibility, exchange listing, stock-token purchases, and token-holder entitlements as separate decisions. Robinhood's [crypto availability page](https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/coin-availability/) distinguishes its trading product from Wallet availability.

If the intended product would distribute equity exposure, treasury returns, or stock-token rewards to holders, its structure needs jurisdiction-specific legal review before an offering. No such entitlement is part of this creative proposal.

## Should the collectible be a 404?

Assuming “404” means a fungible token linked to NFTs: the original [Pandora ERC-404 repository](https://github.com/Pandora-Labs-Org/erc404/blob/main/README.md) calls its implementation experimental and warns that the referenced next version is not production-ready. [DN404](https://github.com/Vectorized/dn404) uses linked ERC-20 and ERC-721 contracts and also describes experimental risks. [ERC-7631](https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-7631) documents dual-nature token pairs and synchronization considerations.

Illustrative mechanics only: if one NFT corresponds to 1,000 fungible units, a wallet with 2,500 units may have two NFTs when NFT synchronization is enabled. Selling 600 units leaves 1,900 and may remove one NFT. The actual ID, burn/transfer behavior, exemptions, and NFT-skipping rules depend on the implementation. A fraction of fungible tokens is not automatically shared legal ownership of a particular rare cat.

This creates a product conflict: fans may become attached to one cat, while routine token trading can affect the linked NFT. Recommendation: use “404: thought not found” as the creative theme immediately. Prefer a conventional collectible for the first collection. If the linked-token mechanic is essential, prototype DN404 separately before committing collectible identities or supply.

Before a funded launch, verify the chosen chain's wallet and marketplace support; simulate threshold crossings, selected NFT transfers, exemptions, rare-ID handling, pool transfers, gas limits, and recovery paths; then obtain review of the exact integrated contracts. An upstream audit does not validate custom code. Do not present hybrid mechanics as a guarantee of liquidity or a stable floor price.

## Immediate deliverables and remaining decisions

This package supplies a research-backed direction, cast, six scripts, a 30-day experiment plan, an art concept board, and a provisional token architecture recommendation. It does not launch a channel, create a coin, mint NFTs, establish rights, or secure a listing.

Next decisions: confirm the Robinhood-related program; choose primary audience/language; select art direction; establish production budget; validate audience response; only then specify any collectible economics and contracts.
