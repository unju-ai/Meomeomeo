## Review focus

Does MEO's urgency read immediately? Is the pause before MO funny or too long? Does the punchline work without needing to know the characters? Does the closing hold earn its length?

The frame sequence follows the existing storyboard. The generated door hardware and door state are imperfect, as already noted in the production pack. Replace those with one consistent background and animated door before final production. This edit deliberately retains the approved concept images rather than pretending they are finished animation.

The presentation uses ivory margins to preserve the storyboard compositions. Final social video should be composed for full-height vertical scenes once character motion is produced. Replace the temporary voices with chosen performances, animate the tiny mouth shapes, refine the sonic signature, and re-time captions to the final audio.
